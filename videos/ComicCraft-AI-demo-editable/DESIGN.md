# ComicCraft AI demo — brand reference

## Overview
The source is a local Chinese comic workspace. Large dark panels contain chapters, a seven-step completed pipeline, character references, structured writing tabs and the generated comic. Real captured screens carry product claims; the six original comic panels carry the visual payoff. Screenshots were captured afresh through Microsoft Edge because CLI capture of localhost returned 403. No files from either blocked capture are used.

## Colors
- Ground: #0a0a0f, the source app background.
- Product panel: #12121a; divider: #242430.
- Primary action: #4f46e5, recurring brand/button violet.
- Text: #f0f0f0; secondary: #9ca3af.
- Success: #6ee7b7, source completed workflow.
- Comic stage: #f6f1e8, sampled from the supplied six-panel comic sheet; used with sans-serif, not an invented paper/serif identity.
- Warm accent: #a67739, derived from comic lamps and caption labels.

## Typography
The live UI uses Segoe UI / Microsoft YaHei. Screens remain pixel captures. Film copy uses the OFL-licensed Noto Sans CJK SC regular font, locally subset and shipped as assets/fonts/comiccraft.woff2. Film heading 80–104px; support 34–42px; story captions 42–48px; legal credit 28px. The source logo is separately captured at3× density.

## Elevation
The source has 1px dividers, 12–16px radii and subtle surface shifts. Film screens remain flat and front-facing. No 3D tilt, glow or fake glass. The framing stage is a light neutral canvas so the actual dark product has visible edges.

## Components
- Real 1600×1000 chapter screenshot and its actual outline/storyboard/dialogue tab states.
- Real six-agent navigation with source/version cards and installed SkillsMP names.
- Actual source header lockup, captured from its DOM element.
- Shared character sheet and six square comic originals; external subtitles leave artwork unchanged.
- Adapted agent-cursor registry graphic with the real captured click coordinates.

## Do's and Don'ts
Do show the same story across source, writing tabs and the result. Do vary the camera between full workspace, skill panel and full-size comic. Do keep all claims factual and show the current collaborative mode. Do use cut/clip reveals and short camera pushes with stable text holds.
Do not invent controls, show unattended desktop generation, imply measured speed gains, rewrite the live UI, simulate unobserved progress, animate every title in the same way, use decorative particles or use a brand mark docked throughout.
